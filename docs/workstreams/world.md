## Backlog (top = next)
_Owner direction 9, 2026-11-25 (design bible): **the basics first.** Character customization, zombie survival, a generated map, and landmarks with loot come before any polish. This list was cut to those on 2026-11-25 with the owner; the old "Later" list (Ashgrove and Emberlight polish) was removed, see git history if one is ever needed again. **Follow-ups in unfolded `world.d/` notes written before 2026-11-25 are parked** unless they are a bug or serve one of the four basics. Order: a bug players hit, then this list top down. Every item that shows something follows `docs/art_bible.md` as it is now: the sculpted body (section 3), `SurfaceTex` painted detail and the clear sunny days of #219. Before and after shots at noon and night in every PR (section 12)._
