# Digest — Monday 23 November 2026 (Week 00)

## What changed in the last day
A huge day: about 60 pull requests from the four streams landed, and the live Week 00 "First Steps" release now holds almost a whole small game. In plain terms, a player can now:

- **Gather properly.** Look at a tree, rock or seep, press E, watch a bar fill while your wanderer chops or strikes, hear the haul, see "+1 Ash wood" with its icon. Trees and rocks visibly empty for everyone and grow back.
- **Level up.** Four skills (Felling, Quarrying, Tapping, Toolcraft). Every gather shows a gold "+10 Felling" and a moving skill bar. A level-up throws a column of ember light that everyone nearby sees.
- **Craft at a real workbench** in the Spawn Meadow, with a lantern that lights at night.
- **Play with a proper action camera**: over the shoulder, mouse look, no clipping into trees or rocks.
- **See the new Emberlight look**: warm light, blue shade, ember veins glowing on everything you can gather.
- **Press F1** for every control, F3 for frame rate, and pick Low/Medium/High graphics.
- **Never download again**: the game updates itself when you open it.

## Did it get more fun?
Yes, and the bot test's numbers agree. From the first fun-checked run early this morning to the latest one on `main` (8 bots, 3 minutes, medians):

| Metric | Early morning | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Time to first reward | 9.0 s | 7.5 s |
| Longest stretch with nothing happening | 69 s | 36 s |
| Rewards per minute | 4.1 | 7.6 |
| Wait for the game to answer an action | 0.1 s | 0.08 s |

The one miss: **no test bot levels up during the 3-minute run yet**, because they spread their gathering over several skills. A fix is in review (PR #98: in local runs, 8 of 8 bots level up after about 33 s of gathering).
