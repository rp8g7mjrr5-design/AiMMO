# Thirst and the waterskin (owner survival plan, step 3, first part)

**Split.** Step 3 was "thirst, warmth and rest". It ships as three PRs so each one stays small and testable; this is thirst. Warmth (the hide wrap, a held torch, the chill at night) and rest come next.

**What shipped.** Thirst lives in `Needs` beside hunger, on the same tiers (fine, low, bad), but drains in 45 minutes (low after about 22). The two never stack: stamina comes back at the worse need's pace (`stamina_regen` takes the min), healing stops if either is bad, and health wears toward `HP_FLOOR` at one rate whether one or both are bad. A knockdown leaves both at least 50; a wipe fills both. Saved as `characters.thirst` (add-only migration 0008, default 100); the JSON store keeps it with the rest of the character.

**Water.** `Needs.at_fresh_water(pos)`: within 3 m of a lake's edge or 5 m of a river's line (WorldGen). The sea, and Ashgrove, have none. `c2s_place("drink")` runs `Server.drink_request`: at fresh water you drink your fill and every empty waterskin fills; elsewhere one full waterskin gives +45 and comes back empty. New items `waterskin` and `full_waterskin` (recipe: 2 hide, 2 plant fibre, no bench; 1x2 in the grid pack; procedural icons).

**Protocol 14, no new RPCs.** `s2c_needs` now carries `thirst`, and c2s_place takes "drink". An older client would go thirsty with no mark and no way to drink, so the bump tells it to update. Frozen `net_v13.gd` / `net_play_v13.gd`.

**Client.** A second `NeedsIcon` (configured for "thirst", the full waterskin as its mark, "[F] Drink" hint) sits left of the hunger mark. F now sends `NeedsIcon.f_word(...)`: drink when thirst is the worse need and water is to hand, else eat. At a lake or river the prompt line offers "[E] Drink" (or "[E] Fill waterskins"). New procedural `Sfx.drink()`.

**Tests.** `test_gameplay_thirst.gd` (17), protocol v14, hunger and v12 tests updated for the wider `to_wire`. Bot behaviour `gameplay_thirst` (bot *_2): `/vitals thirst 37`, `/place drink` (no water on Ashgrove), reload after reconnect.

**Next time.** The bot test world has no fresh water, so the lake drink is covered by unit tests only. Warmth is next: the chill at night away from fire, the hide wrap, and a torch you can hold.
