- **New travellers.** Every character is now one sculpted body instead of a stack of blocks: real shoulders, a full jaw, painted eyes and brows, a beard you can see from across the meadow, and clothes in layers with straight hems (a leather jerkin over your dyed shirt, a belt with a pouch and a knife, turned-down boot cuffs). Pick a hood and you get a scout's cloak to go with it. Your build, face, hair, beard, cap or hood and every dye from the appearance window all carry over. The first time a look appears it takes a couple of seconds to make, then it's saved.
