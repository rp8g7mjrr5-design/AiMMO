### Meet the new folk
- **Everyone has a brand new look:** big round heads, googly eyes, long droopy noses, bushy brows, noodle arms with big hands, and short legs stomping about in big boots. Every option in the character window still works: builds, heights, skin, faces, all six hair styles, beards, hoods and felt caps, and every dye.
- **Faces are clean now.** The grainy pixel noise on faces and shirts is gone.
- **Little legs, quick steps.** Your survivor scampers along with short fast steps, and their feet stay planted on the ground.
- **The Zombai got the same treatment:** the same folk, but dead. Ash-grey skin, blank eyes, slack open mouths, drained clothes and the wound that killed them. You'll know one when you see it.
- Survivors carry a little pack and bedroll, so you can pick out your friends from behind.
